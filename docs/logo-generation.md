# Illustrated profile logo

Created with the built-in image generation tool, using `public/profile.jpg` as the identity reference. The original photograph remains the About-page portrait.

The generated circular illustration is used in `public/profile-logo.png` and resized for the PNG browser icons and Apple touch icon. Transparency outside the circle is preserved.

## Saved assets

- `public/profile-logo.png`: 512 × 512 website logo.
- `public/favicon-32.png`: 32 × 32 browser tab icon.
- `public/favicon-192.png`: 192 × 192 browser icon.
- `public/apple-touch-icon.png`: 180 × 180 Apple touch icon.

## Generation prompt

Use case: style-transfer.
Asset type: circular personal website logo and browser favicon.
Input image: the attached photo is the edit target and identity reference.
Primary request: create a Studio Ghibli-style hand-drawn anime illustration of the person in the photograph, cropped to a centered head-and-shoulders avatar.
Preserve the recognizable face, short tousled black hair, thin dark rectangular glasses, calm friendly expression, and navy hooded jacket. Use warm natural colors, gentle cel shading, delicate clean outlines, and softly painted watercolor textures. Simplify the lake, green mountain, and cloudy sky from the photograph into a quiet background inside the circle.
Composition: square 1:1 canvas, one circular portrait filling the canvas with a tiny 2% transparent margin. Make the face large and clearly readable at favicon size; include the entire hair silhouette, ears, neck, and shoulders. Keep the face centered, with a relaxed slight smile. The illustration and its background must be completely contained inside a crisp perfect circle, with genuine transparency outside that circle.
Constraints: one person only, preserve identity rather than inventing a generic anime face. No letters, text, jacket branding, watermark, ornamental frame, or extra objects. No photorealism, no 3D rendering.
